# Bundled translation data

The bundled locale files are based on Axolotl-PM/PocketMine-MP `resources/translations/*.ini` at commit `77f832da28e5b52996207ca5a8d8b54fd827b77e`. The files cover Bulgarian, Chinese (Simplified), German, English, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Latvian, Dutch, Russian, Spanish, Turkish and Vietnamese. Locale codes were mapped to Bedrock resource-pack IDs. Translation keys with trailing spaces were normalized to match the pinned English catalog.
